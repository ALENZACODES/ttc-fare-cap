Fare Cap Line

Is the TTC's 47-trip fare cap worth chasing?

Since September 1, 2026, adults who tap the same card or device all month pay $3.30 per paid trip, and after 47 paid trips in a calendar month the rest are free, so a month never costs more than $155.10. The adult monthly pass ended August 31, 2026, but the Adult 12-Month Pass is still sold at $143.00 a month on a 12-month contract. Ride the line to see which one wins.

How it was made

Built with Google Gemini Canvas, then rebuilt and tested by hand. Gemini's subway-line idea is kept, but its first plan put break-even at "Station 43" (43 trips cost $141.90, still under $143, so it's 44), and a later edit broke its script. The math now lives in model.js and is checked against a separate implementation for every trip count from 0 to 120.

Educational, not financial advice. Simplified: it assumes adult fares paid with one PRESTO card, debit or credit card, or device all month. Switching cards or devices splits your trip count, cash fares ($3.35) don't count toward the cap, and transfers within two hours aren't new paid trips. The 12-Month Pass is a PRESTO contract paid every month. Post-secondary riders who tap pay the $3.30 adult fare; the student view assumes those taps count toward the cap. Check ttc.ca before you choose.
